# Notes for Claude

## This is the `bonk` branch: Gloomtide (survivors-style run mode)
- Served at /bonk/ (built by the deploy workflow on `main`, like /side/). `main` stays the MMO; don't merge this into it.
- Entry `src/run/main.ts` (index.html boots it). Code lives in `src/run/`: `data.ts` (all balance: `RUN`, weapons, tomes, items, heroes, enemies, bosses, `enemyHpScale`, `spawnRate`, `xpToNext`), `run.ts` (`Run`: the whole sim, DOM free), `render.ts` (same pixel pipeline as the MMO renderer), `sprites.ts`, `input.ts`, `run.css`. It reuses `pixel.ts` sprites and `map.ts` collision; the MMO files are still in the tree but unused here.
- Classes: each hero has an auto attack from Lv 1 (`HEROES[job].basic`, weapon slot 0) and 4 base abilities that unlock on their own at `ABILITY_LEVELS` (2/4/6/10, slots 1-4, `Run.checkUnlocks`). Level-up cards only upgrade owned abilities or offer passives. Each class has its own passive pool (`PASSIVES[job]`, about 10, max level per passive); passives give stats and/or a special (`PSpecial`, read with `Run.sp()`). No shared tomes.
- Job advancement (`BRANCHES`, `ADVANCE_LEVELS` = 10/15/25): at Lv 10 you pick one of 2 branches per class (Warrior: Berserker/Guardian, Archer: Ranger/Marksman, Mage: Pyromancer/Stormcaller). Each tier has `swaps` ([old, new]): the new ability takes the old one's slot and keeps its upgrades (`Run.replaceWeapon` carries level, damage ratio, count/area/pierce deltas, haste; the old one goes to `retired` for the end screen). Each tier also adds 2 passives to the card pool. Kinds: arc, arrow (with `spread` = fan fired at once), bolt, orbit, storm, nova, axe, pool, aura, beam, meteor (`Run.falling`), summon (wolves, `Run.allies`), rain (a lasting zone of falling arrows, a `Pool` with `style: 'rain'`, ticks every 0.25 s). Weapon flags `fire`/`shock`/`burn`/`leech`/`slow`/`shot` are what passives key off (Ignite/Combust need fire, Overload needs shock, Headshot needs shot).
- Class resource + ultimate (MMO style, ported from the MMO's Focus/Deadeye): every class has a resource that fills to `CORE_MAX` and an ultimate on F (`CLASS_CORE`: Warrior Rage/Battle Cry, Archer Focus/Deadeye, Mage Mana/Arcane Surge); at Lv 10 the branch's `core` replaces it (Bloodrage, Divine Shield, Call of the Pack, Deadeye, Firestorm, Storm Avatar). Resource builds from hits of the matching tag (`gain.tag`: melee/shot/nature/fire/shock/any), kills, damage taken or regen, capped at `CORE_RATE` per second (`Run.addCore`). `Run.activateCore/updateCore/endCore`, `coreDmg/coreHaste`. Core passives per tier: coreGain/coreDur/corePower; Marksman keeps the MMO Deadeye talents (Trained Eye, Split Shot, Prolonged Focus, Second Nature, Trueshot keystone: no timer, 7 Focus per empowered arrow, crits refund 10). AUTO and the pause-menu toggle (`autoCore`, localStorage `gloomtide-autocore`) fire it when ready.
- King Jelly lair (port of the MMO boss, `src/run/jelly.ts`, `JellyFight`): the door (`Run.lairDoor`) is on the map from the start; E to enter whenever you want (`enterLair` stashes the world, swaps in `generateLair(0)`; the run clock is paused inside via `runT` = t - `lairSpent`). Replaces the old 5:00 Bog Warden (5:00 now only posts a reminder). One HP pool (`JELLY.hpBase * hpGrowth^level` at entry, ~3 min fight) shared by 1/2/4 slimes (splits at 80%/50%, `damage()` divides by sqrt(alive)). Crowned slime hops (styled telegraphs 'wave'/'landing'/'spout' with `pct` of max HP, `slimed`, `goo`), each landing emits gapped rings (`emitRings`, rest until the last ring is `waveRest` out); rings can't be jumped (`JELLY.jumpRings` false), they slow and add Slimed (10 = death). Split-offs hop/charge and leave goo (roots, `Run.rootFor`; bumps root too; +30% damage while rooted). Wild phase: crowned slime anchored in the middle with nonstop 4-ring pulses and evenly spread glob rain; minis only charge wall to wall toward `cleanSpot`. Run-mode lair has no brazier pillars (turned to floor in `JellyFight`); the renderer puts torches on walls facing the room (`torchSide`, `SP.torch`) with a faint floor glow. Win: Royal Jelly item (`bossOnly`), 3 free chests, 2 levels of EXP, full heal and ultimate; golden portal (E) back out. Pause menu has Restart fight while inside. Test: `npx tsx tools/jellytrial.ts <job> <level> [runs] [god]` (AUTO loses at any level, like the MMO; god mode shows fight length).
- EXP is granted on kill for now (`RUN.xpOnKill`; set false to bring back gem drops).
- Loop: abilities auto-fire, items come from chests (unlimited; gold chests cost `chestCost` rising each open, elite/boss chests are free). Level-ups offer 3 cards with rolled rarity (luck shifts it), 3 rerolls per run. Shrines: stand inside for `SHRINE_TIME` for a boon pick. Space jumps over bodies (not shots or slams). Final boss at 10:00; killing it wins (the 5:00 mini boss is now the optional King Jelly lair). Overtime makes foes tougher.
- Balance check: `npx tsx tools/runsim.ts <job> [runs]` (AUTO bot; Q toggles it in the browser too). Current state: the bot nearly always wins (10-13 min), so the early game is on the easy side now that abilities are free. Deadeye kills the final boss fastest, Warrior the slowest.
- Best result per hero is saved in localStorage `gloomtide-best`.

## MMO notes (shared code)

- Owner: Kelly. She works on Windows and plays the game through GitHub Pages. Do not use em dashes in user-facing text or docs.
- Stack: Vite + TypeScript, plain Canvas 2D (no game framework). Isometric view, action combat (WASD + mouse aim), AUTO pilot with manual override.
- Characters: 3 save slots (`idle-rpg-char-0..2` in localStorage), jobs Warrior/Archer/Mage defined in `JOBS` (data.ts). Skills are data-driven by `SkillType`; implement new types in `Game.castSkills`. Sim per job: `npm run sim -- 4 archer`.
- World: one connected map (`generateWorld` in map.ts), zones in `ZONES` (data.ts), mobs are tagged with a zone and can't cross Gate tiles (pass `mob = true` to map collision/path helpers). Bosses live in lairs (`generateLair`, separate map; `Game.enterLair/exitLair` swap the map and keep world mobs) and are summoned at the lair's circle in order (`bossesCleared`). Summon offerings: `ZONES[z].offering` + `state.items` (empty for now, future item system). The old stage/kills fields in state are unused leftovers.
- Boss difficulty is intentional (Souls-like): %max-HP boss hits, phases, enrage, flasks, imperfect AUTO dodging. Validate changes with `npx tsx tools/bosstrial.ts <job> [zone]` (AUTO should lose at equal level; skilled should usually win) and `npm run sim -- 30 warrior` (AUTO-only journey: boss 1 ~5h, boss 2 ~11h, boss 3 needs a human).
- Boss 1 (King Jelly, `bossSplit` in `ZONES`) splits at each phase: 1, 2, then 4 slimes sharing one HP pool (`SPLIT` in data.ts: smaller, faster, softer hits; multi-slime hits have diminishing returns). `Game.splitGroup`, `splitSlime`, `checkPhase`; killing the pool kills every slime. The original is the crowned main slime (`isMainSlime`) and aims right at the player; split-offs aim via `chaosPoint` (lead the player using `pVel`, or random spots nearby) with jittered timing so attacks don't stack. In phases 1-2 the main slime hops around the room (landing telegraphs have `style` 'wave' for the crowned slime / 'landing' for split-offs: faint outline, fill grows with the jump's travel time, dotted line from the slime in the air; 'wave' adds a second outline) (`updateMainHopper`, `roomSpot`: random open spots at least 220 from you, marked slam landing); each landing sends out slime `waves` (expanding rings with `SPLIT.waveGaps` safe gaps, hit once, and a hit slows you to 50% for 2 s via `slowUntil`; rings per landing `SPLIT.waveCount` = 2 in phase 1, 3 in phase 2, `waveSpacing` apart, each with gaps shifted from the last). It won't jump again until its last ring is `SPLIT.waveRest` of the way out. Every hit from the crowned slime (rings, slams, cut-offs, charges, globes) adds a Slimed stack (`addSlime`, `slimeStacks`, shown under the boss bar); `SPLIT.waveLethal` (10) in one fight kills you. Lair has P2/P3 summon buttons for testing (`summonBoss(phase)`, `skipToPhase`). AUTO slides toward a gap (`waveDodge`). Its specials are `cutoff`/`charge`/`cone`. Split-offs hop (`updateHopper`, `SPLIT.kit` = charges + circle barrages), leave goo (`Game.goo`, 7 s) and bumping them or standing in goo roots the hero (`rootPlayer`). No grace by design (Kelly's call): `SPLIT.rootImmune` is 0, goo that roots you is used up (every puddle you're touching splats away), bumps re-root with no immunity, and hits while rooted deal +30%. Mid-air drops skip the spot you stand on, so every root is avoidable. Phases at 80% / 50% (`phaseAt` in `ZONES`) so the four-slime phase is the long one. In the last phase (the wild phase, `SPLIT.wild`) the main slime leaps to the arena centre and bounces in place (`updateAnchored`) while the minis go wild: they stop jumping and only charge (`SPLIT.wild.kit`), aimed at the cleanest floor in the room (`cleanSpot`: best of 24 open spots by distance from goo and from where the other minis are heading; not the player), specials ~3x as often, charges run wall to wall (`chargeLen` 3000, clipped by `chargeLength`) at 800 speed, `gooMax` 280, red aura. The crowned slime also rains slime nonstop (`spout`, one small glob (r 45, 2 s fall) every `SPLIT.wild.rainEvery` 0.08-0.17 s (~16 on the field; spawned independently of the bounce, several per frame if needed), spread evenly: each glob takes the best of 8 room spots by distance from globs already falling, none aimed at you; telegraph `style` 'spout', each lands on its own timing, no goo, each hit adds Slimed). Trail drops (charges and hops) are `SPLIT.trailEvery` (85) apart so there is always a gap to slip through between puddles. Meanwhile the crowned slime sends 4-ring pulses (`emitRings`, `waveCount[2]`) from the middle nonstop: a new set as soon as the last ring of the previous set is `waveRest` of the way out. The globes are off (`SPLIT.wild.globes`); when on they drop in a checkerboard of 2x2-tile squares (`SPLIT.globeCell`, telegraph kind `square`); each volley flips which squares get hit (`globeParity`), so players can plan. Each square shows a darkening shadow and the globe falling (`drawFallingGlobes`). AUTO heads for the nearest safe square (`Game.safeSpots`). Telegraphs with the same `volley` id hit at most once. A hit that skips a threshold still does every skipped split. AUTO steers around goo (`avoidGoo`/`sticky`). Boss attacks are clamped to the arena (`clampArena`, `chargeLength`). Current state (Kelly wants it this hard): at equal level the skilled bot dies in phase 2 (the double rings); at +3 levels it usually reaches phase 3 and dies there (phase 3 is meant to be the main show); +6 levels wins.
- Dying to a boss (browser only, `game.offerRetry` set in main.ts) waits on a choice (`retryZone`, `awaitingChoice`, `#deathbox`): `retryBoss(phase)` (full HP and flasks, fresh boss at once, optionally starting in phase 2 or 3 with its HP at that threshold) or `respawnNow()`. The sim/bosstrial leave `offerRetry` false and auto-respawn as before.
- Collision: `fits` (map.ts) samples a grid no coarser than half a tile, because bosses are wider than a tile (a corners-only check let them straddle pillars and freeze). `Game.depenetrate` pushes any mob that overlaps a wall back out, and blocked mobs try turning to slide around corners. Charges and hop landings use the walking radius (`m.r * 0.8`).
- Space ability per job (`JOBS[j].evade`, tuning in `EVADE`, 10 s cooldown): Warrior Parry (`parryUntil`; the next hit or root is ignored and `counter()` hits everything nearby for 3x ATK), Archer Sprint (`sprintUntil`, +60% speed for 3 s), Mage Blink (240 units toward movement or aim, needs floor and line of sight, breaks roots). `Game.useEvade`; AUTO uses it while dodging (`autoEvade`). Attack is click or J (Space no longer attacks).
- Prediction: `cutoff` boss move (all bosses' later phases and the split-off kit) puts a line of circles across where you're running plus one further ahead (boxes you in if you stand still); main bosses' `slam` leads your movement 40% of the time. Both use `pVel`.
- Art style: pixel art (Kelly's reference: warm isometric pixel art with dark outlines, bushy highlight-clump trees, earthy palette). Default on; Info > Testing tools switches to the old classic vector art (`localStorage idle-rpg-pixel` = '0', `pixelmode` event). The world canvas is drawn at ~380 game px wide (`block` CSS px per game px, camera snapped to whole pixels, `k` = 0.25 game px per world unit, tiles are 32x16 diamonds) and scaled up with `image-rendering: pixelated`. Text, bars, boss bar and minimap go on a crisp overlay canvas (`#overlay`, `onOverlay`). Sprites are generated procedurally in `src/pixel.ts` (cached canvases: `groundTile`, `stoneTile`, `leafyTree`, `pineTree`, `boulder`, `wallBlock`, `slime`, `hero`, `weapon`, horns/crown) and drawn with `Renderer.blit` (anchor + pixel snap). Gates (`barrier`), braziers (`brazier`) and allies (`spirit`) are sprites. Vector layers (telegraphs, goo, waves, effects, projectiles, shadows, altars) and the big set pieces (lair doors, cave mouth, exit portal) are drawn on a scratch canvas and snapped to hard pixels (`beginCrisp`/`endCrisp`/`crisp`: alpha quantized to 4 steps; `__perf.crisp` shows its cost). Any `outlineText` on the world is redirected to the overlay (`textLayer`). In pixel mode the camera is locked to the hero (no lag) and the walk bob is off; the world is drawn on whole game pixels and the canvas (1 game px bigger on every side, `margin`) is slid by the camera's leftover fraction (`fracX/fracY`, CSS transform) for smooth scrolling. The hero (and its shadow and weapon) is drawn on the overlay at its exact spot (`onOverlay`, `blit` doesn't snap there), so it never jitters; world-anchored overlay drawing is shifted by the same fraction (`overlayWorld`), the HUD isn't. Input reads the container's rect (the canvas moves). The walk is a 4-frame stride (`LEGS`), facing has hysteresis (`face`).
- UI theme: pixel-art (bottom of style.css). Font: Pixelify Sans (OFL, bundled via `@fontsource/pixelify-sans`, imported in main.ts; `--serif`/`--px` point at it, canvas text too). Frames are 12x12 SVG 9-slices as `border-image` (`--f-panel` gold trim with notched corners for panels/windows/toasts, `--f-btn*` raised buttons in wood/red/gold/stone, `--f-inset*` sunken cards, `--f-slot*` skill and menu slots). No rounded corners anywhere; text gets a 2px hard shadow. Character select portraits draw the pixel hero sprite (`drawHeroPortrait`).
- Performance: `window.__perf` shows rolling ms per frame (game/ui/draw). HUD writes go through `setText`/`setStyle` in ui.ts (DOM touched only on change). `findPath` reuses buffers with a binary heap; keep per-frame allocations out of the renderer.
- Power comes mainly from level (`LEVEL_POWER`, data.ts); Growth upgrades are % multipliers. Boss HP is per zone (`BALANCE.bossHpMult[]`).
- Talents: `TALENT_TREES` (data.ts), state.talents, `talentStats`/`talentBlocked` (stats.ts), procs in game.ts (`damage`, `playerAttacks`, `tickDots`, `startSkill`, `hurtPlayer`). Mage has mana (`MANA`) and Arcane Charges. Boss HP per zone assumes the talent points you'd have by then; the sim and bosstrial auto-assign talents.
- Spec cores: each spec should feel like its own class. Marksman = Focus/Deadeye (`FOCUS`, `Game.activateDeadeye`, `empoweredShot`). All talent stats are listed in `TALENT_STATS` and start at 0 (never reference a stat that isn't in that list).
- Echoing Hollow: manual-only cave (`generateCave`, `Game.enterCave/exitCave`, `inCave` forces `auto` false). Cave mobs use zone id `CAVE.zone` (99) and scale to the player's level.
- Monsters come from fixed spawn camps per zone (even level spread). AUTO avoids monsters 4+ levels above you and roams to level-appropriate camps.
- Layout (classic action-RPG HUD, Kelly's pick): unit frame top left (`#unitframe`: pixel portrait `drawUnitPortrait`, name/level, gold/gems/power chips; `#hud-top` under it with AUTO and status effects `#status`), minimap top right (canvas overlay, tiles cached in `Renderer.mini`), zone plate under it (`#stagebar`), and one action bar along the bottom (`#actionbar`: HP orb `#hporb` (pulses under 30%), class resource bar `#resbar` (mana / focus / deadeye), `#skillbar`, EXP line, menu buttons `#tabs` in a 4-column grid, EXP orb `#xporb`). Orbs are stepped pixel circles (`--pixcircle` clip-path). Menus open one big centred window with the same tabs across the top (`#wintabs`, `UI.openWindow/closeWindow`, Esc closes). Under 900px the menu buttons become a right-edge column and the EXP orb hides; phones (`pointer: coarse` or < 720px) stack unit frame, minimap and zone plate at the top, a compact action bar at the bottom, and windows as a bottom sheet; the renderer's `compact` getter moves the boss bar/minimap to match.
- `main` is the isometric game. The old side-scroller lives on the `side-scroller` branch and deploys to /side/; leave it alone unless asked.
